Meet Anthony

Your perspective belongs
at the center of care.

I’m Anthony, a board certified psychiatric mental health nurse practitioner. I work with children, adolescents, and adults navigating mental health concerns and changes in everyday life.

I bring experience in outpatient psychiatry, intensive treatment programs, and substance use care. My approach combines thoughtful medication management with practical coping strategies and shared decision making.

We’ll make room for your questions, talk openly about your options, and build a plan together.

Before we meet

A few helpful details.

Where are appointments available?

Appointments offered through this booking page are virtual visits for patients located in Pennsylvania. Please be physically in Pennsylvania at the time of your appointment.

How do I book an appointment?

Select any “Book” button on this site to open Anthony’s direct Grow Therapy invite link. Complete the booking steps there. Your appointment is confirmed through Grow, not on this website.

Do you accept my insurance?

Insurance participation and appointment availability can change. Check your specific plan through the booking link or with Grow Support before scheduling. Coverage and out of pocket costs depend on your benefits.

Will medication be part of my care?

Medication may be considered after an individualized evaluation. We’ll discuss your goals, preferences, and appropriate options together.

Is this website for patient messages?

This site provides information and a link to booking. Please use your established patient communication channel for clinical questions or follow up.
